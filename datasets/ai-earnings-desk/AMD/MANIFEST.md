# AMD: AMD earnings corpus

Downloaded 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-07 (local time). Window: SEC filings and earnings releases dated on or after 2023-10-01.

**Licence notes:** SEC filings and the XBRL company-facts JSON are public records. Investor-relations (IR) files are © AMD; they are not included in this repository, so do not republish them.

## Contents

- **SEC filings:** 12 (3 10-K, 9 10-Q; amendments excluded), each saved as the primary document (`sec/*.htm`).
- **Financial_Report.xlsx:** 7 of 12 published by SEC. SEC's filing folders stop including this workbook for filings made from mid-May 2025 onward, so the other 5 were rebuilt from the same filing's R pages (SEC's rendered statement pages, listed in its FilingSummary.xml) and saved as `*_Financial_Report_rebuilt.xlsx`: 10-Q FY2025-Q2, 10-Q FY2025-Q3, 10-K FY2025, 10-Q FY2026-Q1, 10-Q FY2026-Q2.
- **How faithful the rebuilt workbooks are:** the converter was checked against two filings that do have SEC's workbook (NVDA 10-K FY2025, MSFT 10-Q FY2025-Q3). Sheet names, merged cells, all 2,746 numbers and their number formats, and all short text matched, except one cell that SEC itself cuts short. Long note-text cells hold every word of SEC's version, but the spacing differs (SEC's version also loses some text). The converter is `../scripts/rebuild_financial_report.py`, and the check is `../scripts/compare_financial_reports.py`.
- **XBRL company facts:** `xbrl/AMD_companyfacts.json` (every reported XBRL fact for the company, snapshot of 2026-10-06).
- **IR files:** 80 (12 html, 56 pdf, 12 xlsx), in `ir/<fiscal period>/`; quarters FY2023-Q3 to FY2026-Q2. Kinds: 3 annual_report, 6 other, 24 press_release, 12 slides, 24 supplemental_tables, 11 transcript.
- **IR source:** https://ir.amd.com/financial-information/quarterly-results. Quarterly links came from AMD's financial-results page (plain HTML). The FY2023 and FY2024 annual reports and the infographics were found through Internet Archive copies of AMD's own pages, then downloaded live from AMD's CDN (d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net)
- **IR gaps:** AMD publishes no CFO commentary or prepared remarks. Transcripts start at FY2023-Q4, and infographics stop after FY2024-Q4. The FY2023 annual report is AMD's Form 10-K as a PDF. Two PDFs carry a wrong embedded title (the FY2023-Q3 press release and the FY2024-Q4 infographic), but their content is correct.

**Files:** 105, 143,019,329 bytes in total.
