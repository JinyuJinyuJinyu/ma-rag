# INTC: Intel earnings corpus

Downloaded 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-07 (local time). Window: SEC filings and earnings releases dated on or after 2023-10-01.

**Licence notes:** SEC filings and the XBRL company-facts JSON are public records. Investor-relations (IR) files are © Intel; they are not included in this repository, so do not republish them.

## Contents

- **SEC filings:** 12 (3 10-K, 9 10-Q; amendments excluded), each saved as the primary document (`sec/*.htm`).
- **Financial_Report.xlsx:** 7 of 12 published by SEC. SEC's filing folders stop including this workbook for filings made from mid-May 2025 onward, so the other 5 were rebuilt from the same filing's R pages (SEC's rendered statement pages, listed in its FilingSummary.xml) and saved as `*_Financial_Report_rebuilt.xlsx`: 10-Q FY2025-Q2, 10-Q FY2025-Q3, 10-K FY2025, 10-Q FY2026-Q1, 10-Q FY2026-Q2.
- **How faithful the rebuilt workbooks are:** the converter was checked against two filings that do have SEC's workbook (NVDA 10-K FY2025, MSFT 10-Q FY2025-Q3). Sheet names, merged cells, all 2,746 numbers and their number formats, and all short text matched, except one cell that SEC itself cuts short. Long note-text cells hold every word of SEC's version, but the spacing differs (SEC's version also loses some text). The converter is `../scripts/rebuild_financial_report.py`, and the check is `../scripts/compare_financial_reports.py`.
- **XBRL company facts:** `xbrl/INTC_companyfacts.json` (every reported XBRL fact for the company, snapshot of 2026-10-06).
- **IR files:** 56 (12 html, 44 pdf), in `ir/<fiscal period>/`; quarters FY2023-Q3 to FY2026-Q2. Kinds: 3 annual_report, 5 other, 12 prepared_remarks, 24 press_release, 12 slides.
- **IR source:** https://www.intc.com/financial-info/financial-results. Links came from Intel's financial-results page (plain HTML, one block per quarter), cross-checked against the earnings event pages, the press-release list and the presentations page. Files are on Intel's CDN (d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net) and HTML releases on intc.com
- **IR gaps:** Intel posts no call transcripts (webcast only) and no separate CFO commentary; its joint CEO/CFO 'Earnings Call Comments' are filed here as prepared_remarks. There are no PPTX, DOCX or XLSX files, and infographics stop after FY2024-Q3. Annual report dates are the SEC filing dates. Text extracted from the FY2025 annual report PDF has every word doubled.

**Files:** 81, 105,432,661 bytes in total.
