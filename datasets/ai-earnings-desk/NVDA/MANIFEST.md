# NVDA: NVIDIA earnings corpus

Downloaded 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-07 (local time). Window: SEC filings and earnings releases dated on or after 2023-10-01.

**Licence notes:** SEC filings and the XBRL company-facts JSON are public records. Investor-relations (IR) files are © NVIDIA; they are not included in this repository, so do not republish them.

## Contents

- **SEC filings:** 12 (3 10-K, 9 10-Q; amendments excluded), each saved as the primary document (`sec/*.htm`).
- **Financial_Report.xlsx:** 6 of 12 published by SEC. SEC's filing folders stop including this workbook for filings made from mid-May 2025 onward, so the other 6 were rebuilt from the same filing's R pages (SEC's rendered statement pages, listed in its FilingSummary.xml) and saved as `*_Financial_Report_rebuilt.xlsx`: 10-Q FY2026-Q1, 10-Q FY2026-Q2, 10-Q FY2026-Q3, 10-K FY2026, 10-Q FY2027-Q1, 10-Q FY2027-Q2.
- **How faithful the rebuilt workbooks are:** the converter was checked against two filings that do have SEC's workbook (NVDA 10-K FY2025, MSFT 10-Q FY2025-Q3). Sheet names, merged cells, all 2,746 numbers and their number formats, and all short text matched, except one cell that SEC itself cuts short. Long note-text cells hold every word of SEC's version, but the spacing differs (SEC's version also loses some text). The converter is `../scripts/rebuild_financial_report.py`, and the check is `../scripts/compare_financial_reports.py`.
- **XBRL company facts:** `xbrl/NVDA_companyfacts.json` (every reported XBRL fact for the company, snapshot of 2026-10-06).
- **IR files:** 58 (12 html, 46 pdf), in `ir/<fiscal period>/`; quarters FY2024-Q3 to FY2027-Q2. Kinds: 3 annual_report, 12 cfo_commentary, 12 press_release, 12 slides, 13 supplemental_tables, 6 transcript.
- **IR source:** https://investor.nvidia.com/financial-info/quarterly-results/default.aspx. The document list came from the public JSON feeds the site itself uses (financial reports, press releases, events). Files are on NVIDIA's CDN (s201.q4cdn.com) and press releases on nvidianews.nvidia.com
- **IR gaps:** NVIDIA posts call transcripts (FactSet-produced) only from FY2026-Q1, and publishes no XLSX, PPTX, DOCX or press-release PDF files. The FY2024-annual PDF is a 2026-02-19 re-upload of the 2024 report. For FY2024-Q3 to FY2026-Q2 the slide decks were posted 2 to 14 days after each release. The annual report PDFs bundle the Form 10-K and proxy statement.

**Files:** 83, 206,648,272 bytes in total.
