# MU: Micron earnings corpus

Downloaded 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-07 (local time). Window: SEC filings and earnings releases dated on or after 2023-10-01.

**Licence notes:** SEC filings and the XBRL company-facts JSON are public records. Investor-relations (IR) files are © Micron; they are not included in this repository, so do not republish them.

## Contents

- **SEC filings:** 12 (3 10-K, 9 10-Q; amendments excluded), each saved as the primary document (`sec/*.htm`).
- **Financial_Report.xlsx:** 7 of 12 published by SEC. SEC's filing folders stop including this workbook for filings made from mid-May 2025 onward, so the other 5 were rebuilt from the same filing's R pages (SEC's rendered statement pages, listed in its FilingSummary.xml) and saved as `*_Financial_Report_rebuilt.xlsx`: 10-Q FY2025-Q3, 10-K FY2025, 10-Q FY2026-Q1, 10-Q FY2026-Q2, 10-Q FY2026-Q3.
- **How faithful the rebuilt workbooks are:** the converter was checked against two filings that do have SEC's workbook (NVDA 10-K FY2025, MSFT 10-Q FY2025-Q3). Sheet names, merged cells, all 2,746 numbers and their number formats, and all short text matched, except one cell that SEC itself cuts short. Long note-text cells hold every word of SEC's version, but the spacing differs (SEC's version also loses some text). The converter is `../scripts/rebuild_financial_report.py`, and the check is `../scripts/compare_financial_reports.py`.
- **XBRL company facts:** `xbrl/MU_companyfacts.json` (every reported XBRL fact for the company, snapshot of 2026-10-06).
- **IR files:** 34 (34 pdf), in `ir/<fiscal period>/`; quarters FY2024-Q1 to FY2026-Q4. Kinds: 12 prepared_remarks, 10 press_release, 12 slides.
- **IR source:** https://investors.micron.com/quarterly-results. The document list came from the public JSON feeds on investors.micron.com (financial reports, press releases, events), read with the 10-second pause its robots.txt asks for. Files are on Micron's CDN (s25.q4cdn.com)
- **IR gaps:** Micron's HTML pages are behind a Cloudflare bot check, so its 12 HTML press releases were not downloaded. PDF copies cover FY2024-Q1 to FY2026-Q2. The FY2026-Q3 and FY2026-Q4 releases exist only as those blocked pages, so they are missing here (Micron also furnishes its earnings releases to the SEC on Form 8-K). Micron publishes no CFO commentary, supplemental tables, spreadsheets, transcripts or standalone annual report; its annual report is the Form 10-K. Micron's FY2026 10-K is not filed yet, so the FY2026-Q4 rows have no report date in INDEX.csv. By Micron's rule (its fiscal year ends on the Thursday closest to August 31) that quarter ends 2026-09-03.
