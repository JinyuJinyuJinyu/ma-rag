# AVGO: Broadcom earnings corpus

Downloaded 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-07 (local time). Window: SEC filings and earnings releases dated on or after 2023-10-01.

**Licence notes:** SEC filings and the XBRL company-facts JSON are public records. Investor-relations (IR) files are © Broadcom; they are not included in this repository, so do not republish them.

## Contents

- **SEC filings:** 12 (3 10-K, 9 10-Q; amendments excluded), each saved as the primary document (`sec/*.htm`).
- **Financial_Report.xlsx:** 6 of 12 published by SEC. SEC's filing folders stop including this workbook for filings made from mid-May 2025 onward, so the other 6 were rebuilt from the same filing's R pages (SEC's rendered statement pages, listed in its FilingSummary.xml) and saved as `*_Financial_Report_rebuilt.xlsx`: 10-Q FY2025-Q2, 10-Q FY2025-Q3, 10-K FY2025, 10-Q FY2026-Q1, 10-Q FY2026-Q2, 10-Q FY2026-Q3.
- **How faithful the rebuilt workbooks are:** the converter was checked against two filings that do have SEC's workbook (NVDA 10-K FY2025, MSFT 10-Q FY2025-Q3). Sheet names, merged cells, all 2,746 numbers and their number formats, and all short text matched, except one cell that SEC itself cuts short. Long note-text cells hold every word of SEC's version, but the spacing differs (SEC's version also loses some text). The converter is `../scripts/rebuild_financial_report.py`, and the check is `../scripts/compare_financial_reports.py`.
- **XBRL company facts:** `xbrl/AVGO_companyfacts.json` (every reported XBRL fact for the company, snapshot of 2026-10-06).
- **IR files:** 27 (12 html, 15 pdf), in `ir/<fiscal period>/`; quarters FY2023-Q4 to FY2026-Q3. Kinds: 3 annual_report, 24 press_release.
- **IR source:** https://investors.broadcom.com/financial-information/quarterly-results. investors.broadcom.com drops every scripted request, so each file was downloaded from its raw Internet Archive copy. The source URL recorded here is the archive URL, which contains Broadcom's original URL. Links came from archived copies of the Quarterly Results and Annual Reports pages; FY2026-Q3 was found by web search
- **IR gaps:** Broadcom publishes only a press release each quarter: an HTML page plus a PDF the site generates. It posts no slides, CFO commentary, separate tables, spreadsheets or transcripts. The annual reports are PDFs of the Form 10-K, dated by their 10-K filing dates.

**Files:** 52, 46,788,287 bytes in total.
