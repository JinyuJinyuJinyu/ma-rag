# MSFT: Microsoft earnings corpus

Downloaded 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-07 (local time). Window: SEC filings and earnings releases dated on or after 2023-10-01.

**Licence notes:** SEC filings and the XBRL company-facts JSON are public records. Investor-relations (IR) files are © Microsoft; they are not included in this repository, so do not republish them.

## Contents

- **SEC filings:** 12 (3 10-K, 9 10-Q; amendments excluded), each saved as the primary document (`sec/*.htm`).
- **Financial_Report.xlsx:** 7 of 12 published by SEC. SEC's filing folders stop including this workbook for filings made from mid-May 2025 onward, so the other 5 were rebuilt from the same filing's R pages (SEC's rendered statement pages, listed in its FilingSummary.xml) and saved as `*_Financial_Report_rebuilt.xlsx`: 10-K FY2025, 10-Q FY2026-Q1, 10-Q FY2026-Q2, 10-Q FY2026-Q3, 10-K FY2026.
- **How faithful the rebuilt workbooks are:** the converter was checked against two filings that do have SEC's workbook (NVDA 10-K FY2025, MSFT 10-Q FY2025-Q3). Sheet names, merged cells, all 2,746 numbers and their number formats, and all short text matched, except one cell that SEC itself cuts short. Long note-text cells hold every word of SEC's version, but the spacing differs (SEC's version also loses some text). The converter is `../scripts/rebuild_financial_report.py`, and the check is `../scripts/compare_financial_reports.py`.
- **XBRL company facts:** `xbrl/MSFT_companyfacts.json` (every reported XBRL fact for the company, snapshot of 2026-10-06).
- **IR files:** 89 (41 docx, 24 pptx, 24 xlsx), in `ir/<fiscal period>/`; quarters FY2024-Q1 to FY2026-Q4. Kinds: 6 annual_report, 12 cfo_commentary, 12 financial_statements, 8 other, 3 prepared_remarks, 12 press_release, 12 slides, 12 supplemental_tables, 12 transcript.
- **IR source:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/. The live earnings pages build their download lists in a script, so links were recovered from Internet Archive copies of those pages and by resolving aka.ms short links. Every file was then downloaded live from Microsoft's CDN (cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com)
- **IR gaps:** www.microsoft.com refuses scripted requests (403), so 138 HTML pages were not downloaded. Most are web versions of the press releases, statements, metrics, transcripts and annual reports, which are all here as DOCX or XLSX. The exception is 44 'performance' pages explaining each quarter's results (company-wide and per segment), which have no file equivalent. The FY2026 annual report is not published yet. Outlook deck file names don't match their release (FY2026-Q1's deck is OutlookFY26Q2.pptx), so each deck was matched by its title slide. The FY2026-Q2 and Q3 prepared remarks are on Microsoft's CDN but not linked from the current pages.

**Files:** 114, 136,639,802 bytes in total.
