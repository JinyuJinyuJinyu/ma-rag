# GOOGL: Alphabet earnings corpus

Downloaded 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-07 (local time). Window: SEC filings and earnings releases dated on or after 2023-10-01.

**Licence notes:** SEC filings and the XBRL company-facts JSON are public records. Investor-relations (IR) files are © Alphabet; they are not included in this repository, so do not republish them.

## Contents

- **SEC filings:** 12 (3 10-K, 9 10-Q; amendments excluded), each saved as the primary document (`sec/*.htm`).
- **Financial_Report.xlsx:** 7 of 12 published by SEC. SEC's filing folders stop including this workbook for filings made from mid-May 2025 onward, so the other 5 were rebuilt from the same filing's R pages (SEC's rendered statement pages, listed in its FilingSummary.xml) and saved as `*_Financial_Report_rebuilt.xlsx`: 10-Q FY2025-Q2, 10-Q FY2025-Q3, 10-K FY2025, 10-Q FY2026-Q1, 10-Q FY2026-Q2.
- **How faithful the rebuilt workbooks are:** the converter was checked against two filings that do have SEC's workbook (NVDA 10-K FY2025, MSFT 10-Q FY2025-Q3). Sheet names, merged cells, all 2,746 numbers and their number formats, and all short text matched, except one cell that SEC itself cuts short. Long note-text cells hold every word of SEC's version, but the spacing differs (SEC's version also loses some text). The converter is `../scripts/rebuild_financial_report.py`, and the check is `../scripts/compare_financial_reports.py`.
- **XBRL company facts:** `xbrl/GOOGL_companyfacts.json` (every reported XBRL fact for the company, snapshot of 2026-10-06).
- **IR files:** 33 (33 pdf), in `ir/<fiscal period>/`; quarters FY2023-Q3 to FY2026-Q2. Kinds: 3 annual_report, 12 press_release, 6 slides, 12 transcript.
- **IR source:** https://abc.xyz/investor/. The document list came from the public JSON feeds on abc.xyz (financial reports, press releases, events). Files are on Alphabet's CDN (s206.q4cdn.com)
- **IR gaps:** The 12 HTML transcript pages on abc.xyz are behind a Cloudflare bot check and were not downloaded; the PDF transcripts here have the same text. Slides start at FY2025-Q1 (none were published before). Alphabet posts no CFO commentary or separate supplemental tables; the tables are inside the earnings release. The CEO's prepared remarks are on blog.google, not the IR site, so they were left out. Annual report dates are the proxy release dates.

**Files:** 58, 52,188,351 bytes in total.
